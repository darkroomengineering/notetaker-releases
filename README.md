# NoteTaker for Mac

NoteTaker records and transcribes your meetings on your Mac. It needs a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 13 or later; recording your microphone and calls needs macOS 15.

## Install

1. Download [NoteTaker.dmg](https://github.com/darkroomengineering/notetaker-releases/releases/latest/download/NoteTaker.dmg) and open it.
2. Drag NoteTaker onto the Applications folder in the window that opens, then open it from Applications. A waveform icon appears in the menu bar.
3. Sign in with your Darkroom Google account when the browser opens. Other Google accounts are refused.
4. Grant what macOS asks for: Microphone, Notifications and Screen Recording (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording, then quit NoteTaker from its menu and open it again).
5. On your first Aside call, macOS asks whether NoteTaker may control Aside. Click Allow, so the call is matched to its calendar event.

## Updates

NoteTaker updates itself. It checks once a day, downloads new versions in the background and installs them while nothing is recording. To check now, choose Check for Updates… in the menu. To be asked before installing, turn off Update Automatically in Settings → General.

Coming from a build that did not update itself: download it once more from the link above and replace the old app. macOS then asks for Screen Recording and Microphone again, and may ask to let NoteTaker use your keychain; click Always Allow.
